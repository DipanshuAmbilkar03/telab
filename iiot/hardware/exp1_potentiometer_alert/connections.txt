Experiment 1: Alert Message using Potentiometer
Connections (actual hardware, ESP32)

ESP32 (USB cable) -> Laptop (for power, upload and Serial Monitor)

Potentiometer 10K (placed on breadboard):
  Middle pin (signal)  -> GPIO 34 of ESP32
  One side pin (red)   -> 3.3V of ESP32
  Other side pin (orange) -> GND of ESP32

Same wiring is in diagram.json in this folder.
