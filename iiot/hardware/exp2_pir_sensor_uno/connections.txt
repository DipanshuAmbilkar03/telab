Experiment 2: Interfacing PIR Sensor with Arduino Uno
Connections (actual hardware, Arduino Uno)

Arduino Uno (USB cable) -> Laptop (for power, upload and Serial Monitor)

PIR motion sensor:
  GND pin -> GND of Arduino Uno
  VCC pin -> 5V of Arduino Uno
  OUT pin (signal) -> Pin 2 of Arduino Uno

LED:
  Anode (long leg)   -> Pin 13 of Arduino Uno (through a 220 ohm resistor)
  Cathode (short leg) -> GND of Arduino Uno

Same wiring is in diagram.json in this folder.
