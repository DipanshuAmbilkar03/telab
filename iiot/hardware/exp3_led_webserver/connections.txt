Experiment 3: LED Web Server (Monitoring and Controlling a CPS System)
Connections (actual hardware, ESP32)

ESP32 (USB cable) -> Laptop (for power and upload; phone/laptop on the same Wi-Fi hotspot opens the web page)

LED 1:
  GPIO 12 of ESP32 -> 220 ohm resistor -> Anode (long leg) of LED 1
  Cathode (short leg) of LED 1 -> GND of ESP32

LED 2:
  GPIO 14 of ESP32 -> 220 ohm resistor -> Anode (long leg) of LED 2
  Cathode (short leg) of LED 2 -> GND of ESP32

Same wiring is in diagram.json in this folder.
